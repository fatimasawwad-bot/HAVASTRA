THE IDEA BEHIND THE WORLD

A place for every story beneath the stars.

HAVASTRA is an imagined coastal village with a very human purpose: to make room for curiosity, care, connection and discovery. Wander from a sunlit library to a cosmic bakery, from world cuisines to the wonders of space, while dedicated sanctuaries offer care and new beginnings.

THE MEANING OF OUR MARK

A symbol of wisdom and welcome.

Our celestial owl represents wisdom and the protection of living creatures. The open book stands for learning; its sheltering shape suggests home and belonging. Moonlight and stars speak to exploration, while the village's food and cultural experiences bring warmth and connection to the wider story.
